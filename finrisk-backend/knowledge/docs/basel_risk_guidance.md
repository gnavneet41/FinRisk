# Basel risk guidance (FinRisk summary)

Summary written for FinRisk of the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision framework (Basel III). Consult the official BIS publications for the authoritative text.

## Scope: banks only

The Basel framework sets prudential requirements for internationally active banks. Its ratios and minimums are bank regulatory requirements. They are not benchmarks for non-financial companies, and FinRisk must not apply them when assessing a company such as Apple Inc.

## Capital requirements

Banks must hold minimum capital relative to risk-weighted assets: Common Equity Tier 1 of at least 4.5%, Tier 1 capital of at least 6% and total capital of at least 8%, plus a capital conservation buffer of 2.5% of risk-weighted assets. A leverage ratio of Tier 1 capital to total exposure of at least 3% acts as a backstop.

## Liquidity requirements

The Liquidity Coverage Ratio requires a stock of high-quality liquid assets at least equal to expected net cash outflows over a 30-day stress period (at least 100%). The Net Stable Funding Ratio requires available stable funding to be at least equal to required stable funding over a one-year horizon (at least 100%).

## Relevance for FinRisk

The general ideas carry over to company analysis: liquidity risk is about meeting short-term outflows, and solvency risk is about the cushion of equity against losses. The numeric minimums do not carry over.
