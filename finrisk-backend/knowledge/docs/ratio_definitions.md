# Ratio definitions used by FinRisk

These definitions describe what each ratio measures. They are the formulas used by the FinRisk Calculation Engine. They contain no benchmarks.

## Current ratio

Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities. It compares the assets expected to be turned into cash within a year with the obligations due within a year. A value below 1 means current liabilities are larger than current assets.

## Quick ratio

Quick ratio = (cash + short-term investments + accounts receivable) / current liabilities. It is a stricter liquidity test than the current ratio because it leaves out inventory and other current assets that may take longer to turn into cash.

## Cash ratio

Cash ratio = (cash + short-term investments) / current liabilities. It is the strictest liquidity test: it counts only cash and short-term investments.

## Debt-to-equity

Debt-to-equity = total debt / total shareholders' equity. It shows how much borrowed money is used relative to the owners' capital. A value above 1 means debt is larger than equity.

## Debt ratio

Debt ratio = total debt / total assets. It shows the share of assets financed with interest-bearing debt.

## Liabilities-to-assets

Liabilities-to-assets = total liabilities / total assets. It includes all liabilities, not only debt, such as accounts payable and deferred revenue.

## Interest coverage

Interest coverage = operating income / interest expense. It shows how many times operating profit covers interest payments. It needs a disclosed interest expense figure; FinRisk does not estimate it when it is missing.

## Gross margin

Gross margin = gross profit / revenue x 100. It is the percentage of revenue left after the direct cost of the goods or services sold.

## Operating margin

Operating margin = operating income / revenue x 100. It is the percentage of revenue left after cost of sales and operating expenses such as research and development and selling, general and administrative costs.

## Net profit margin

Net profit margin = net income / revenue x 100. It is the percentage of revenue left after all expenses, including taxes.

## Return on assets (ROA)

ROA = net income / total assets x 100. It shows how much profit the company earns on its asset base. FinRisk uses period-end assets; average assets would require the prior-year balance.

## Return on equity (ROE)

ROE = net income / total equity x 100. It shows profit relative to shareholders' equity. It becomes very large when equity is small, for example after large share buybacks.

## Asset turnover

Asset turnover = revenue / total assets. It shows how much revenue is generated per unit of assets.

## Inventory turnover

Inventory turnover = cost of goods sold / inventory. It shows how many times inventory is sold and replaced during the year.

## Receivables turnover

Receivables turnover = revenue / accounts receivable. It shows how many times receivables are collected during the year.

## Operating cash flow to current liabilities

CFO to current liabilities = operating cash flow / current liabilities. It shows how much of the short-term obligations one year of operating cash flow could cover.
